Barnabos

Privacy policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

Barnabos is a personal executive assistant intended to help its owner with scheduling and coordination.

Information accessed and how it is used

With Google account authorization, Barnabos can access the account email address, calendar events, and free/busy information. Event information may include titles, times, locations, descriptions, meeting links, organizers, and attendees. Barnabos uses this information to answer scheduling questions, prepare agendas, check availability, and help manage calendar events, including creating or deleting them.

Storage and retention

Google authorization credentials are stored in the local Barnabos profile on the owner's computer. Calendar information may also appear in local conversation history, logs, or backups, depending on how the assistant is used and configured. Those local records remain until removed; Google retains calendar events separately according to its own settings and policies.

Sharing and processing

Barnabos sends calendar requests to Google to provide its features. Calendar details or summaries may be sent through the owner's configured messaging service, such as Apple's iMessage via BlueBubbles, or shared with configured agents when the owner enables or authorizes that coordination. Google may send event invitations to invited attendees. The configured assistant currently uses a local AI model to process requests.

Barnabos does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising. Its use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Access and deletion

You can revoke Barnabos's access through your Google Account connections. Revoking access does not automatically delete existing calendar events or local files. To remove retained information, delete the local authorization credentials, relevant conversation history, logs, and any backups that contain it.

Website and contact

This informational website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may receive technical request information, such as an IP address, when the site is visited. This site does not request Google authorization or display calendar data.
