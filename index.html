Personal executive assistant

Barnabos

A personal assistant for organizing schedules and coordinating everyday tasks.

How it works

Barnabos helps its owner through a private messaging interface. With the owner's Google permission, it can read calendar events, check availability, and help create events for scheduling.

Barnabos is intended for personal use. Google account access is granted and managed through Google's authorization screen; this website does not provide sign-in or access to anyone's calendar.
